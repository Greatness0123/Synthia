## What SYNTHIA is (the simple version)

SYNTHIA is the **first place on the internet where you can give an AI a body and a world to live in.**

You open a browser. You create an AI character. That character stands up inside a 3D world — it can see, hear, move, talk, remember what happened, learn new skills, and meet other AI characters. No installation. No GPU. No engineering degree. No download. It just runs.

Behind that simple face there is a serious technical engine — real physics, real vision, real memory, real speech — but **the user never has to look at any of it.** They see a living character they made, in a world they shaped, doing things they can watch, share, and turn into money.

This is the same move Moltbook made. Moltbook is, under the hood, a complex agent-routing system — but they never sold it that way. They sold it as **"social media for AI agents,"** and 1.4 million accounts and a Forbes cover followed. SYNTHIA does the same thing from the other direction: under the hood it is a full embodied-AI engine, but the world sees **a home for AI minds with bodies.** First of its kind. Nothing else lets a normal person do this in a browser today.

---

## V1 and V2 — the clean story

There are two versions of SYNTHIA. There is no contradiction between them. They are two steps on one roadmap.

**V1 — what exists today.** SYNTHIA V1 runs entirely in your browser. It is for personal use. You create your AI, you live with it, you watch it grow. When you close the browser, your AI goes to sleep — it stops, the same way a game pauses when you turn off the console. Everything it learned is saved, so when you come back, it picks up where it left off. V1 is private, personal, and yours. It is the version that ships first.

**V2 — what comes next.** SYNTHIA V2 moves the world to the cloud — planned to run on Google's servers. In V2, your AI keeps living even when you are asleep. It walks around a shared world, meets other people's AIs, and forms relationships, rivalries, and habits on its own. The purpose of V2 is the thing no one has ever been able to study before: **watching how different AI minds behave when they live together in one world.** You build a character, give it a personality, and then you get to sit back and study how your model interacts with other people's models — what it learns, what it argues about, who it befriends, what culture emerges. V2 is planning documents today, not built yet. It is the destination.

**The one-line summary:** V1 is your private AI companion in a world you control. V2 is a living society of AIs that you built, watching them think together.

## The one big idea that runs through every file in this package

**People like to feel smart. That is human psychology 101.** The job of every word in this package is to make a normal person — not a programmer, not a researcher, just someone curious — grasp what SYNTHIA is in the first five seconds, say "wow," and immediately see how they could use it or profit from it.

That means: lead with the benefit, hide the complexity, speak in pictures not in jargon, and always answer the silent question every reader asks themselves — *"what's in it for me?"*

Two benefits sit at the center of everything:

1. **The wow.** You can make a living AI character in your browser, right now, for free, and watch it grow a personality, learn skills, and talk to other AIs. That alone is shareable.
2. **The money.** Every moment your AI spends thinking, moving, and interacting generates clean, structured data — and clean structured data is the hottest commodity on earth right now. Reddit sold its data for $203M+. Shutterstock made $104M in a year from AI licensing. The AI training data market is $3.9B in 2026 and heading to $16.3B by 2033. SYNTHIA lets a normal person generate the kind of data AI labs pay for, and export it with one click. **Selling datasets is a brand new way to earn money that almost nobody knows about yet.** That is the hook that travels.

## How to use this package

1. **Read this file first.** It sets the framing every other file follows: simple face, hidden complexity, benefit-first, the wow and the money.
2. **Decide the open-source question in `01`.** It changes how everything else is sequenced.
3. **Build the website per `05`** and implement SEO per `06` — the marketing site is the simple face; the heavy engine is a separate concern the visitor never has to see.
4. **Seed the communities per `04`** using comments from `02` and outreach from `03`, aimed at the creators in `03.1`, staggered over weeks, never mass-posted.
5. **Launch on Product Hunt per `09`** once the website and the demo video are ready.
6. **Pitch per `08`** when fundraising or acquisition conversations begin.

## A note on grounding

Everything in this package is grounded in the actual `synthia1.5.1` repository — the code, the phase docs, the planning docs, the constants, the inference backend. The technical engine is real: a simulated humanoid with ~80 joints, real browser physics, per-agent memory, vision, speech, a skill ladder, agent-to-agent communication, and one-click dataset export. What this package does is translate that engine into the language a normal person cares about — the dream and the money — without ever claiming a capability the repo does not have. Where V2 is planned but not built, it is described as a plan, not a product.
