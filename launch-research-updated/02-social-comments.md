## How to use these (the rules)

- **Never post the same comment twice in the same community.** Variety is what keeps you from looking like a spammer.
- **Always fit the room.** In a creative space, use Section A. In a money space, use Section B. In a "what's new" space, use Section C. When someone asks "how is this different from X," use Section D.
- **Reply, do not broadcast.** The best results come from leaving one of these as a *reply* in a relevant conversation, not as a standalone post. Standalone posts feel like ads; replies feel like a person sharing something they found.
- **One link, ever.** End with `[synthia.online]` once. Do not repeat it. Do not add a second link. The curiosity does the rest.
- **Be a person first.** If you are in a thread, participate in the conversation genuinely, then drop the comment where it fits. People click links from people they are already talking to, not from billboards.
- **Stagger.** Do not post ten in a day. One or two a day, across different communities, over weeks. The goal is steady curiosity, not a spike that gets flagged.
- **Watch what lands.** The comments that get upvotes or replies tell you which hook travels best for which audience. Double down on those. Retire the ones that go quiet.
