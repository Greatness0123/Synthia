# 03 — Outreach Templates for Creators and Communities

These are first-touch messages for reaching out to content creators, community leaders, and online groups who would care about SYNTHIA — the first place where you can give an AI a body and a world to live in, right in your browser, and export what it learns as data you can sell.

The principle behind every message: **lead with them, not with you.** Open by showing you actually know their work. Ease into SYNTHIA only after they feel seen. Never send a message that could go to a thousand people unchanged — if it could, it will be ignored by all of them.

Every template is a starting point. Personalize the bracketed parts with something real about the specific person before you send. Do not reuse the same template on two people in the same community.

## Rules for all outreach

- **Personalize the bracketed parts with something real.** A message that could go to anyone goes to no one. Spend two minutes on the specific detail before you send.
- **One message per person.** Do not follow up more than once. If they do not respond, they are not interested this week — move on, try again in a few months with something new.
- **Lead with them, always.** Even the direct approach opens with one genuine line about their work. It is what separates a respectful ask from a billboard.
- **Offer support concretely.** "I will provide any info and assistance" only lands if you mean it and follow through. Have a demo, assets, and answers ready before you send.
- **Never mass-send.** These are templates, not a mail merge. Five thoughtful messages beat five hundred copy-pasted ones, every time.
- **For Discord and Telegram, respect the culture.** Join first, participate, then approach a mod/admin. Never drop a link in a channel without permission. A community that feels respected becomes your best amplifier; one that feels spammed bans you and tells other communities.
- **Track who you contacted.** Keep a simple list: name, platform, date sent, response. You will need it when you follow up later and when you expand to the creator list in `03.1-creator-list.md`.
