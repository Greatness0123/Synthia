# 08 — Pitch Deck Content (Slide-by-Slide Text)

> **How to use this file.** This is the spoken text for each slide, written to be presented in 10–12 minutes with questions. A separate design pass turns it into visuals. The deck follows the same principle as the rest of the launch package: **lead with the benefit, plant the first-of-its-kind flag, sell the dream and the money, and let the engine be the proof — not the pitch.** The tone is warm, confident, and specific. No hype words, but no self-flagellation either. The honesty in this deck is the healthy kind — "here is what is real today, here is where it is going" — not the kind that makes an investor wonder why you are spending a slide apologizing.

## Deck-level rules

- **Lead with the benefit on every slide.** The first thing an investor sees on each slide is what SYNTHIA does for someone, not how it works inside. The engine is the proof, shown briefly on slide 6, not the headline.
- **Plant the first-of-its-kind flag, clearly and without hedging.** "The first place on the internet where you can give an AI a body and a world to live in, in a browser" is a true statement. Say it with confidence. Do not water it down. "First" is the word that makes an investor lean in.
- **Sell the dream and the money, in that order.** The wow (slides 1–3) earns the right to talk about the money (slide 4). Never lead with the market size — lead with the living character, then show that the character's exhaust is a sellable product in a real market.
- **Be honest about stage, not self-flagellating.** V1 is real and working — say so with pride. V2 is planning — say so plainly. The embedding model that ranks memory relevance is a placeholder the architecture is ready for — mention it only if asked, in one sentence, as "one wiring step away from done," not as a slide-length apology. Investors respect a founder who knows exactly what is finished and what is next. They do not respect a founder who spends a slide undermining their own product.
- **No hype words.** Not "revolutionary," not "game-changing," not "seamless," not "next-generation." The deck's credibility comes from specificity — real numbers, real capabilities, a real repo — not from adjectives.
- **The deck is 12 slides.** Resist adding a generic TAM slide pulled from a report; the market size is already on slide 4, grounded in real numbers tied to SYNTHIA's actual data product. Resist adding a "team" slide unless there is a team to show — a solo founder deck is honest and common; an inflated team slide is worse than none.
- **Speaker notes are the real content.** The slides are sparse and visual by design. Practice the speaker notes. The spoken version carries the substance; the slides carry the feeling.
- **If an investor wants to go deep, point them at the repo and at file 07.** The full functionality and applications, with source-file grounding, live in `07-functionality-and-applications.md`. The deck is the door. The repo is the proof.
