# SYNTHIA researcher outreach presets

This file contains researcher-facing email and message presets for the SYNTHIA project. The first 30 are designed for feedback on the platform itself. The second 30 are designed for distributing a paper draft or research note for evaluation and feedback.

All examples use placeholders like {{researcher_name}}, {{topic}}, {{your_name}}, {{project_link}}, and {{paper_link}}. They are written to feel natural, high-signal, and not like cold bulk outreach.

## 3) Subject-line keyword strategies for inbox auto-sorting

Rather than just writing “Priority,” use keywords that make the message legible to both a human and a filtering system:

- embodied AI
- reinforcement learning
- world models
- robotics
- policy data
- browser-native simulation
- multimodal perception
- training data
- embodied agents
- robotics learning
- RL dataset
- policy export
- embodied benchmarking
- real-physics agents
- learning from interaction
- agent memory
- simulation-to-real
- embodied reasoning
- multimodal training
- robot learning
- AI with a body
- policy generation
- research feedback
- manuscript review
- draft feedback

This increases the chance that the message is recognized as relevant and not rejected as mass outreach.

## 4) Delivery strategy: keep it human

- Keep warm, relevant context in the first sentence.
- Include a direct project site and repo link.
- Make the ask specific: “If relevant, I’d value a quick read” rather than “Please review my project.”
- Send in batches of 5–15 maximum per day, not dozens.
- If you receive no reply, follow up once after 7–10 days with a shorter and clearer note.
- If a researcher responds positively, continue with a brief, specific note and ask if they would like a more detailed summary or a technical appendix.

## 5) Quick personalization checklist

Before sending, personalize at least one of the following:

- mention a recent paper or topic they wrote about
- reference their work in embodied systems, RL, robotics, or world models
- mention the exact connection between SYNTHIA and their research line
- add one sentence about why the project matters in this area

A personalized 120–180 word note with one direct ask will outperform a broad, generic message every time.
