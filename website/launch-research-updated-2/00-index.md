## What SYNTHIA is (the simple version)

SYNTHIA is a world where an AI mind with a body learns to live — in your browser, no install.

You open a page. An AI is placed into a 3D world. It has a body with real physics, it sees the same world you see, it hears, it moves, it talks, and it remembers what happened. It does not run on a script. Each second it observes its situation, decides what to do, and acts — on its own. Over time it learns skills, one step at a time, from barely balancing to walking to navigating on its own. You can put more than one in the same world and they see, hear, and talk to each other — but under the rules of physics, so a wall between them garbles the words the way real sound does.

Your part is to shape what it does. You build the world around it — objects, terrain, obstacles. You give it a goal, or you let it explore freely. And while it is acting, you can inject a thought into its mind — a plain sentence like "try to reach the red box" — and it folds that into what it does next. You are not remote-controlling it. You are setting the conditions and steering its attention. Everything it experiences — every observation, decision, action, memory, and outcome — you can export with one click as a clean dataset. That data is exactly what AI labs pay for.

Behind that simple face there is a serious engine — real physics, real vision, real memory, real speech, a skill ladder, agent-to-agent communication, dataset export — but the user never has to look at any of it unless they want to. The technical detail lives on separate pages for the people who ask. The front of the project stays plain.

This is the Moltbook move in the other direction. Moltbook is, under the hood, a complex agent-routing system, but they sold it as "social media for AI agents," and 1.4 million accounts and a Forbes cover followed. SYNTHIA is, under the hood, a full embodied-AI engine, but the world sees a place where an AI mind with a body learns to live — and where a normal person can shape that, watch it, and turn what it learns into data. First of its kind. Nothing else lets a normal person do this in a browser today.

## V1 and V2 — the clean story

There are two versions of SYNTHIA. There is no contradiction between them. They are two steps on one roadmap.

**V1 — what exists today.** SYNTHIA V1 runs entirely in your browser. It is for personal use. You place an AI into a world, you shape what it does, and you watch it learn. When you close the browser, the AI stops — everything it learned is saved, so when you come back it picks up where it left off. V1 is private, personal, and runs on your machine. It is the version that ships first.

**V2 — what comes next.** SYNTHIA V2 moves the world to the cloud — planned to run on Google's servers. In V2, your AI keeps living even when you are away. It inhabits a shared world, meets other people's AIs, and you get to study something no one has been able to study before: how different AI minds behave when they live together in one world — who they talk to, what they argue about, what habits and patterns emerge. V2 is planning documents today, not built yet. It is the destination.

**The one-line summary:** V1 is your private AI in a world you control and shape. V2 is a shared world of AIs you can study over time.

## The one big idea that runs through every file in this package

The job of every word in this package is to let a normal person — not a programmer, not a researcher, just someone curious — grasp what SYNTHIA is in the first few seconds, and immediately see how they could use it or profit from it. That means: lead with what it does, keep the language plain, speak in pictures not in jargon, and always answer the silent question every reader asks themselves — "what's in it for me?"

Two things sit at the center of everything:

1. **What it is.** A world where an AI mind with a body learns to live, in your browser, for free. You shape the world, give goals, inject thoughts, and watch it learn skills and interact with other AIs. That alone is worth sharing.
2. **What you can earn from it.** Every moment the AI spends observing, deciding, moving, and interacting generates clean, structured data — and clean structured data is a hot commodity right now. Reddit sold its data for $203M+. Shutterstock made $104M in a year from AI licensing. The AI training data market is $3.9B in 2026 and heading to $16.3B by 2033. SYNTHIA lets a normal person generate the kind of data AI labs pay for, and export it with one click. Selling datasets is a way to earn money that most people do not know about yet. That is the hook that travels.

## How to use this package

1. **Read this file first.** It sets the framing every other file follows: simple front, hidden complexity, benefit-first, what it is and what you can earn.
2. **Decide the open-source question in `01`.** It changes how everything else is sequenced.
3. **Build the website per `05`** and implement SEO per `06` — the marketing site is the simple front; the heavy engine is a separate concern the visitor never has to see.
4. **Seed the communities per `04`** using comments from `02` and outreach from `03`, aimed at the creators in `03.1`, staggered over weeks, never mass-posted.
5. **Launch on Product Hunt per `09`** once the website and the demo video are ready.
6. **Pitch per `08`** when fundraising or acquisition conversations begin.

## A note on grounding

Everything in this package is grounded in the actual `synthia1.5.1` repository — the code, the phase docs, the planning docs, the constants, the inference backend. The technical engine is real: a simulated humanoid with ~80 joints, real browser physics, per-agent memory, vision, speech, a skill ladder, agent-to-agent communication, and one-click dataset export. What this package does is translate that engine into the language a normal person cares about — without ever claiming a capability the repo does not have. Where V2 is planned but not built, it is described as a plan, not a product.

One honesty note that runs through the whole package: the AI does not know you exist. It does not see you, it does not wave at you, and it does not have a relationship with you. What you have is a steering surface — you build the world around it, you give it goals, and you inject thoughts into its mind. That is the real, honest value, and it is more interesting than false intimacy. Every file in this package holds that line.
